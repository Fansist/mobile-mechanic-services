About Us

A garage on wheels, built on trust

Certified, licensed mobile technicians arrive in fully equipped service trucks and fix cars on the spot at homes and workplaces, from computer diagnostics to brakes, suspension, and transmission service.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

A few honest answers before you call.

Do you really come to my location?

Yes. Mobile Mechanic Services is a service-area business based in Mission Valley, the technician and fully equipped truck travel to your home, workplace, or roadside location anywhere in San Diego rather than you bringing the car to a shop.

What kinds of repairs can be done on-site?

Computer diagnostics, brakes and rotors, suspension and struts, transmission service, fuel pump replacement, oil changes and tune-ups, and general auto repair are all handled on location. Call ahead with your symptoms and vehicle details so the technician can arrive prepared.

What are your hours?

Open seven days a week, roughly 7:30am, 8:30pm on weekdays and 7am, 5-6:30pm on weekends. These are approximate hours based on public listings, so it's worth calling ahead to confirm same-day availability.

How do I get a quote or schedule a visit?

Call (619) 765-9319 and describe your vehicle and the issue. Since every job and vehicle is different, pricing and scheduling are handled directly over the phone rather than through an online form.

Are your technicians certified and licensed?

Yes, technicians are certified and licensed mobile mechanics who arrive in a fully equipped service truck built to handle repairs on-site.
